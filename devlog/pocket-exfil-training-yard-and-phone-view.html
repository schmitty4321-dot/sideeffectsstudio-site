Pocket Exfil: a training yard and a better phone view

October 31, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

Pocket Exfil has a lot going on: guards with view cones, locked exits, a backpack that can get too heavy, a pouch you always keep. That's a lot to learn by getting knocked down. This week I worked on two things: teaching new players the game one step at a time, and making it play better on a phone.

The Training Yard

New players now get an offer the first time they open the game: play a training raid of about two minutes first. It takes place on its own small map, the Training Yard, and nothing there can hurt you. It goes through six steps, each with a marker on the map and a short line telling you what to do:

  1. Walk to the marker.
  2. Search a crate, holding still while you search.
  3. Pick up the loot and put it in your bag.
  4. Take down a target dummy.
  5. Crouch behind sandbags.
  6. Run to the green EXFIL and stay inside until the countdown ends.

Each step shows the right controls for how you're playing: keys and mouse on a computer, thumbs and buttons on a phone. After the raid, the hideout shows a few short tips. If you'd rather jump straight in, you can skip it, and you can replay it any time from the gear (⚙) menu. On October 31 I made the step cards stand out more with an orange outline, and made sure Skip training always works.

A camera that zooms by itself

I added auto zoom. Out in the open the camera pulls back so you can see more of the field. Near and inside buildings it moves in closer. In a fight it frames the guards you're facing, and it holds steady while you're shooting so your aim doesn't shift under you. You can switch it off if you prefer a fixed view.

Better on phones

A phone screen is small, and Pocket Exfil is about seeing guards before they see you. So the world is now drawn at 75% size on phones by default, which shows more of the map. If you want a different view, the gear menu has 100%, 60% and 50% too, and the change applies right away, even mid-raid.

I also cleaned up the phone screen: a sharper, more compact HUD, stronger outlines on the soldiers so they stand out from the ground, and quieter scenery so the things that matter are easier to spot.

The biggest fix was a freeze. When a squad of guards first came into view, the game had to prepare all of their pictures at once, and on a phone that could freeze the game for a moment, right when you needed it most. Now the game prepares those pictures a little at a time from the moment a raid starts, so they're usually ready before you've even left the jeep or helicopter. On phones it also uses smaller copies of them, which look the same at phone size and take much less memory.

A couple of smaller touch fixes: the whole bottom of the results screen now counts as the Back to Hideout button, because the button itself was tiny on a phone, and the goal line and labels in the hideout now stay on screen.

Also new

  • A splash screen with a loading bar, then a tap to play.
  • An app icon, so the game looks right when you add it to your phone's home screen.
  • A graphics pass on all five maps: moving water and plants, factory smoke, snow and antenna lights, plus sounds tied to landmarks on the map.
  • On the perks screen, hovering a perk now shows what it does and why it's locked.

What's next

The next big step is playing it on real phones in real hands, and seeing where people get stuck. If something in a raid confuses you or feels unfair, tell me through the contact page.
