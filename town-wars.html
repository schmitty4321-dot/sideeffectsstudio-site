Town Wars

Turn your real town into a kingdom. Its woods, lakes, parks and shops are held by silly gangs: beat them, build on the spots, and grow your castle.

Town Wars is an early test build. It's playable today, but rules, numbers and looks will change, and some planned features (like raids between towns) aren't in yet. This guide covers what's live now.

How to play

  1. Pick your town. Tap Find my town or search for any town by name. The game draws the town inside its real border as a cartoon sticker, with your castle on the town hall.
  2. Look at the spots. Woods, water, fields and parks, rocky ground, shops, landmarks and real places like libraries, diners and post offices become spots on the map. Each one is held by a gang.
  3. Send an expedition. Tap a spot to see its gang, what kind of fighters they are, and whether your army would win. Then send your army.
  4. Fight. When your troops arrive, auto-resolve the battle or watch and fight it yourself.
  5. Build and collect. Win and you can build on the spot: a lumber mill in the woods, a farm on a field, a fishing dock on the water. Buildings fill up over time; tap them to collect.
  6. Grow your castle. Spend wood, stone and gold to upgrade the Castle and its buildings, train new fighters and take on tougher gangs and bosses.

There are four resources: 🌾 food, 🪵 wood, 🪨 stone and 🪙 gold. The goal card in the top-left always shows your next goal and its reward, and its Show button flies the map to the right spot. The info card in the top-right shows your resources, army, Castle level, food balance and the Kingdom, Town, 📍 and 🗺️ buttons.

Your first 10 minutes

A new kingdom starts with 60 food, 60 wood, 30 stone, 30 gold and 6 Pitchfork Peasants. Your Castle makes a little of each resource every minute, but your peasants eat food too, so food matters from the start.

  1. The first goal is Beat the gang in the woods and build a lumber mill. Tap Show on the goal card. The map flies to the nearest woods.
  2. The spot card names the gang (in our test town it was the Raccoon Bandits), explains how they fight, and says something like "Your army (6) would win, losing about 1." That preview is reliable.
  3. Tap Send expedition. It costs a bit of food for the road. Your very first expedition arrives in about 30 seconds; later ones take longer the farther away the spot is.
  4. When the army arrives, choose Watch & fight to see the battle. Tap Rally when the dot is in the middle of the bar.
  5. After the win, tap Build lumber mill. Come back in a minute and tap the mill to collect its wood.
  6. Next goals: build a farm or fishing dock, grow your army to 10, upgrade the Granary, and train your first Catapult Granny.

Open 📋 Quests too. You get 3 daily quests, and a day's play also counts toward your streak.

Battles

Every unclaimed spot is held by one of 30 gangs: the Goose Gang on fields, Raccoon Bandits in the woods, the Duck Mafia on the water, Seagull Shoplifters at the shops, the Pigeon Council at landmarks, the Overdue Book Brigade at libraries, and many more. Everyone who plays the same town sees the same gang on the same spot. Each gang fights in one of four styles:

  • Swarm: lots of weak units. Catapult Grannies' splash hits them twice as hard.
  • Tank: few and very tough. Mascot Knights hit them twice as hard; most other troops do less damage.
  • Ranged: they hit hard early, so bring numbers. Llama Lancers' charge is strong against them.
  • Sneaky: they go for your weakest troops first. Ninja Mail Carriers catch them.

A battle lasts up to 5 rounds. In Watch & fight you can tap Rally each round: a perfect tap in the middle of the drum bar gives +15%, any other tap +8%, and no tap +5%. Each fighter type you brought has one special move per battle, like the Peasants' Pitchfork Charge, the Grannies' Casserole Barrage or the Knights' Shield Wall. Retreat ends the fight as a loss but saves the troops still standing. Bosses get angry below half health and hit harder.

Half of the troops that fall were only wounded and come back after the fight. If you lose, the gang digs in and gets a little stronger at that spot, so it's better to come back with a bigger army than to try again at once. Gangs farther from your castle are stronger, and each town has 3 gold-rimmed spots guarded by a boss that make double output once you win them.

Strategy

Early game: Castle level 1 and 2

  • Watch your food. Six peasants eat 1.5 food a minute, and expeditions cost food for the road. A farm or fishing dock early keeps your army fed. If food runs out, some soldiers desert.
  • Upgrade the Granary and Storehouse early. Each level doubles what they hold (200 at level 1), and Castle upgrades soon cost more wood than a level 1 Storehouse can hold.
  • Build a Quarry on rocky ground if your town has one. If it doesn't, the Trade post (Kingdom panel, More tab) sells stone for 2 gold each, wood for 2 and food for 1.
  • Build on Market stalls for gold. You'll need gold to train and upgrade.
  • Your first Castle upgrade costs 100 wood, 40 stone and 30 gold and happens at once. Each Castle level lets you run 2 more resource buildings (5 at level 1), raises the highest level your other buildings can reach, and grows your territory.

Middle game: Castle 3 and new fighters

  • Castle 3 takes the builders 2 hours, and it brings the Tax Collector, who empties your buildings into your stores while you play.
  • Your territory is the area around the castle (it grows with each Castle level) plus a ring around each building. Expeditions can reach spots inside it, plus the nearest spot of each kind and any spot you've scouted.
  • Build a Scout Post. The 🚲 Bike Scout checks one spot up to 3 km away, the 🍦 Ice-Cream Truck (level 2) reveals spots within 300 m of a point up to 6 km away, and the 🎈 Hot-Air Balloon (level 3) goes anywhere. A scouted spot gives +10% in its fight.
  • Real places unlock new fighters when you build on them: a Llama Barn on a field (Llama Lancers), a Tater Tot Diner at a diner (Lunch Lady Archers), a Bowling Alley at a sports ground, a Post Office (Ninja Mail Carriers, who bring home extra loot) and a Theatre (the Marching Band, who make the whole party hit harder).
  • A Mess Hall on a diner bakes pies (10 food each). A pie rides along on a fight for +10%.
  • Special buildings like the Library (cheaper Castle upgrades), Inn (more army room), Fuel Depot (faster, cheaper expeditions) and Workshop (cheaper building) don't use one of your resource building slots.

Late game: bosses, trophies and guards

  • Pick fights by style: Grannies and Water Balloons for swarms, Knights and Bowling Ball Rollers for tanks, Lancers for ranged gangs, Mail Carriers for sneaky ones.
  • Beat a boss and build a Trophy Hall on a library spot. Each trophy on show adds 3% to its land's resource.
  • A Gang Clubhouse on industrial land lets you hire a gang you've beaten to guard a building for +15% output.
  • Castle 4 takes 3 days and Castle 5 takes 12 days to build, so start the builders and keep playing while they work. The game has 20 goals in all.

Quests and streaks

Each day brings 3 quests (everyone in the same town gets the same 3), like "Collect from your buildings 5 times" or "Win a fight". You can swap one a day, and a chest opens when all 3 are claimed. A weekly board runs Monday to Sunday. Rewards grow with your Castle level, and anything that won't fit in your stores turns into gold. Your streak grows by one for each day you play and gives gold gifts at 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Missing a day isn't a disaster: one missed day a week is covered, and after that the streak drops by half instead of resetting.

Tips

  • Collect before you close the game. While you're away (up to 8 hours) your Castle makes half its usual income and your army eats half as much, and your buildings fill up for you to tap later.
  • Buildings hold 2 hours of output, plus 1 hour per Castle level.
  • The whole army goes on each expedition, and only one expedition can be out at a time, so you can't train while it's away.
  • Short on food for a trip? The spot card offers to buy it with gold.
  • A yellow resource chip means that store is full: spend it or upgrade the Granary or Storehouse.
  • Tap 🗺️ to see the neighbor kingdoms: the real towns around yours, shown as colored patches you can tap.

For parents: location and privacy

Town Wars uses a real map, so here is exactly what it does with location. We checked this against the game's code.

  • Choosing a town: you can type a town's name, and no location is used at all. If you tap Find my town, the phone asks permission to share a rough location. The game rounds it to about 1 km and sends that rounded point to OpenStreetMap's public place-lookup service (Nominatim) to learn which town it's in. It isn't saved. The game saves only the town's name, ID and the town's own center point, never yours.
  • Walking is optional: everything in the game can be played from home. A switch called "Walk to real places" (Kingdom panel, More tab) is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, the phone's precise location is used to show you on the map and to let you plant a banner at a spot within 100 m, between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m. US Central time, up to 5 a day, and not while moving faster than about 30 km/h (so not from a car). A banner gives +25% in that fight.
  • Precise location stays private. While walking is on, the position is kept only in the game's memory. It isn't saved on the device and isn't sent to us or to any lookup service. The map does load pictures of the area you're in, like any map app. The game remembers only whether the switch is on or off.
  • Which places are spots: public things like woods, parks, water, shops, libraries and post offices. Schools, homes and apartments, playgrounds, medical sites, places of worship, cemeteries and police or fire stations are never used as spots. Map data can be imperfect, so if your child walks to spots, go together or agree on places first.
  • Other services: map pictures load from OpenFreeMap, and town borders and the nearby-towns list come from OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (those lookups use points around the town's center, not the player). Like any website, these services see the device's internet address when they're asked for data.
  • Cost and ads: free, with no purchases, no payments and no ads in the game.
  • Other players: there are no accounts, no chat and no messages. Neighbor towns are shown on the map, but you can't contact or fight other players.
  • Saving: your kingdom saves in the browser on your device, one kingdom per town. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it.
  • Age: we rate Town Wars 13+ because it shows a map of your real town and can use location. The game doesn't ask for an age. The fighting is cartoon slapstick: grannies throw casseroles, raccoons steal wood, and beaten gangs "run off".

FAQ

Is Town Wars free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no purchases and no ads.

Do I have to walk around to play?

No. Walking is an optional switch that's off unless you turn it on. Every spot can be won with expeditions from home; walking just lets you plant banners for a +25% boost.

Does the game know where I live?

No. If you use Find my town, a location rounded to about 1 km is sent to OpenStreetMap's lookup once to find the town, and it isn't saved. Your precise location (only with walking on) stays on your phone and is never sent or saved.

Can I play a town I don't live in?

Yes. Search for any town by name. Each town has its own kingdom and save, and the Town button switches between them.

Why can't my expedition reach a spot?

It's outside your territory. Upgrade the Castle, build closer, or scout the spot first. The nearest spot of each kind is always in reach.

My army keeps shrinking. Why?

You're out of food, so soldiers desert. Build a farm or fishing dock, buy food at the trade post, or train fewer troops until your food balance is positive.

Can I attack other players' towns?

Not yet. Neighbor kingdoms show on the map, but raids aren't in the game. Walls are there for when they arrive.

What if my town is missing a resource?

Some towns have no rocky ground or no water. Buy what you lack at the trade post, and goals that need a spot type your town doesn't have are skipped.

Can I start over?

Yes. Kingdom panel, More tab, Restart this kingdom. Your castle spot and daily streak stay; everything else resets.

Who is it for?

We rate it 13+. The humor and cartoon battles suit a wide range of ages, and the rating is about the real-world map and location features.

Play it now

Town Wars runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download. It works best on a phone in portrait.
