Brainrot Gem Quest

Three games in one adventure: mine gems in a brick breaker, refine them in a bubble shooter, then battle bosses and catch them for your crew.

How to play

  1. Walk the trail. Each boss waits at the top of a branching trail. On every row you pick one stop: a Gem Mine, a Bubble Refinery, a quick fight, a treasure, a minigame and more. Choose the stops that give you what you need.
  2. Mine. Bounce the ball to smash walls of gem bricks and dig out rough gems. Special bricks help: Explosive, Lightning, Multiball, Fireball, Wide Anvil and Chain bricks clear big chunks at once.
  3. Refine. In the bubble shooter, match 3 or more of one colour to refine your gems. Gems come in four qualities: Rough, Cut, Flawless and Radiant.
  4. Forge. In the Workshop, drop gems into the sockets of your bat, shield, amulet and crown. Every item level adds a Hero level.
  5. Battle and catch. Tap to parry just before the boss hits, swipe the arrows to strike, and hold to charge. Win, then tap the glowing circles on the capture ring to add the boss to your crew.

Your first 10 minutes

Toc Toc Tronchetto shows you around. Tap a glowing stop on the map to walk there. The first trail is short, only 3 rows, and each later boss adds one more row, up to 8.

Your first real job is to get gems and forge them. Visit a Gem Mine, then open the Workshop from the bottom bar and drop your gems onto your bat. If you're short on time, the FILL & FORGE button fills the empty sockets with your best gems and forges in one tap.

The first bosses are gentler than later ones, so this is the time to learn parrying. Watch the boss wind up, and tap just as the ring closes in. A good parry blocks the hit and sets up a counter. If you lose, the defeat card tells you the single most useful fix, such as "forge your first gear" or "parry more", and often has a button that takes you straight there. You also get one free second wind per fight: Tronchetto gets back up at half health.

New parts of the game open as you beat bosses: the Crew tab at Stage 2, the Store and Brawl at Stage 3, and your Town at Stage 6. Don't forget the DAILY button on the map. It gathers the daily gift, the free Lucky Spin, the Store deals and the Black Market trades in one place.

Strategy

Early game: forge first, fight second

Every boss has a level, and it matches its stage. Your Hero level is 1, plus every item level you've forged, plus Hero Training from the Forge Shop. If the boss is a higher level than you, spend your gems in the Workshop before you fight.

Each gem colour feeds one stat: Ruby is attack, Sapphire is defence, Emerald is regeneration, Topaz is critical hits and Amethyst is magic. Better quality counts for much more: a Flawless gem is worth 1.6 times a Cut one, and a Radiant gem 2.5 times. Forging lots of one colour also builds up its enchantment: Burn (Ruby), Freeze (Sapphire), Lifesteal (Emerald), Double Strike (Topaz) and Arcane (Amethyst). Each gets stronger at three steps.

Items unlock one at a time: the bat first, then the shield, the Gem Amulet and the Gem Crown. Each item has five levels, from Moonlit to Golden, and the highest level you can reach goes up every two bosses. So it's worth forging all your items rather than piling everything into one.

Good early buys in the Forge Shop are the Spare Pickaxe (an extra ball in the mine), the Gem Sack (more refine shots) and Hero Training (+10% hero health and attack per level).

Middle game: buffs, crew and minigames

Stops near the boss can make the fight much easier, but each buff lasts for one boss fight only. The Espresso Bar gives an attack boost, Forge Strike gives defence, Pizza Toss gives gold and health, and the Campfire lets you rest for health or sharpen for attack. A Rich Vein makes your next mine give 50% more gems. Pick these on the rows right before the boss.

Your trails start with six minigames and add one every two stages, from Gem Polish at Stage 3 up to Clock Rhythm at Stage 17. From Stage 4 you can also find Cave-In: an endless mine wall that creeps down a row every few seconds, with bonuses at 50, 100 and 200 rows cleared.

Captured bosses and pack characters join your Crew and work for you, even while you're away. Each one has a job: scouting for gems, fishing for gold, winding the clock for time crystals, growing coffee beans or stargazing for spin tokens. Tap COLLECT on the map to bank what they earned. Benched helpers train toward more stars: 12 hours for ★2 and 24 hours for ★3. Socketing a gem into a crew member as a charm makes their job earn more, and camp buildings bought with gems add 30% per level to one kind of income.

In the Store, a Crew Pack starts at 250 gold and a Rare+ Pack at 900, and each one you buy costs a bit more for the rest of the day. Check the DEALS tab first: it often has a discounted pack.

Late game: the world tour, Brawl and the Grand Journey

After the five Italian bosses, the trail becomes a world tour across 18 countries, from George Washingtoad in the USA to Vikingfjordo Salmonsson in Norway. Each country boss you defeat joins your team. Bosses keep getting tougher, so keep forging, keep your items at the level cap and use every pre-boss buff.

Brainrot Brawl is a two-minute lane battle. You build a deck of four cards from your crew and pay espresso to deploy them. Destroy the rival's tower, or have more tower health left when time runs out. Cheap cards let you defend first and then push back. Your own deck becomes your defence that other players can battle while you're away. Wins earn trophies, gold and gems.

Your Town grows out of the crew camp. Build walls, a cannon, Bean Traps and a Vault to defend your gold, and a Spy Tower, Barracks and a War Drum to raid better. Rival towns can raid you back while you're away and take a crew member to their jail for a day, but you never lose anyone for good: win a revenge raid, pay a ransom or wait.

The Grand Journey (the JOURNEY button on the map, from Stage 2) is a long list of 20 goals along the Roman Road. Milestones pay 500 gold at stop 5, 5 Lucky Spins at stop 10 and a Rare+ pack at stop 15. Stop 20 gives the Journey Pack with La Grande Combination, a Legendary you can't get anywhere else. The later stops are hard on purpose, so treat it as a long-term goal.

Tips

  • Locked Chests cost gem value to open (Rough 1, Cut 2, Flawless 4, Radiant 8). The game warns you before you walk to one you can't afford.
  • Some bosses have twists. With Feints, don't parry the bluff wind-ups. A Ring Shield breaks with a parry or a Gem Burst.
  • Trace the Gem Burst rune neatly in one stroke: a perfect rune hits much harder than a shaky one.
  • On the capture ring, tap the five glowing circles in order. Three wrong taps fail the try, but you get a second chance.
  • Claim the daily gift every day. Day 2 is a Rare+ pack and Day 7 an Epic+ pack.
  • The strip under the map shows your single next goal. Tap it to go there.

For parents

  • Cost: free. There are no real-money purchases. Gold and gems are only earned by playing.
  • Ads: the game shows no ads.
  • Chat: none, and no free text anywhere. Online player names are picked at random by the game from a fixed word list, like "Turbo Sharkolo 42".
  • Online features: Brawl battles run through our own server. Other players never play live against your child: they battle a saved team that the computer plays. The server keeps a random player ID, the random name, trophies, the saved defence team and battle records (kept about 30 days). Other players can see only the random name, badge, trophies and rank, and the defence team when they battle it.
  • Saves: progress is saved in the browser on your device, with a backup copy on our server linked to the random ID, not to a person. If the game crashes, it sends an anonymous crash report with no player ID. Full details are in the privacy policy below.
  • Age: cartoon characters and slapstick fights with no blood. Some jokes have a cheeky theme, such as a shady card dealer and rival towns that "kidnap" a crew member for a day. It suits children who can read short labels, and adults enjoy it too.

FAQ

Does Brainrot Gem Quest cost money?

No. It's free and has no real-money purchases. Everything is earned by playing.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, in your browser's storage, with a backup copy on our server. It saves by itself as you play.

How do I move my game to a new phone or tablet?

In Settings on your old device, get a restore code. It's 8 letters and numbers, works once and lasts 24 hours. Type it into Settings on the new device. Don't share the code with anyone.

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

A different browser, a private window or cleared site data starts a new game. If you still have the old device, get a restore code there. If not, email the studio from our contact page.

Can I play on iPhone or iPad?

Yes. The game is made with phones in mind. Open it in Safari, and use Share, then Add to Home Screen for an app icon.

Can I play offline?

Most of the game can start without a connection after you've played online once. Brawl battles against other players and the cloud backup need the internet; without it, Brawl offers a practice rival.

Why do I keep losing to a boss?

Compare the boss level with your Hero level, forge more gems, and practise parrying. Read the "Fix" line on the defeat card: it names the most useful change.

What do the gem colours do?

Ruby adds attack, Sapphire defence, Emerald regeneration, Topaz critical hits and Amethyst magic.

Is there chat with other players?

No. There's no chat and no way to type messages. Players only see each other's random names and teams.

Play it now

Brainrot Gem Quest runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
