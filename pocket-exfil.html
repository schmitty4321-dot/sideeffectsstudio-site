Pocket Exfil

A top-down extraction shooter with chunky toy soldiers: drop into a raid, sneak or fight past guard squads, grab what you can and reach an exit before time runs out.

How to play

  1. Gear up in the hideout. Pick your camo team (each has its own ability), choose a gun and gear, then press Deploy.
  2. Raid. Ride in, then sneak through tall grass or shoot it out with guard squads. Search caches, lockers and supply drops for loot.
  3. Get out. Reach a green EXFIL and hold still to extract before the timer runs out. Go down and you lose your backpack, but your secure pouch is always kept.
  4. Grow stronger. Sell loot, buy better gear, take contracts, spend perk points as you level up, and take on each map's boss.

Tips

  • On a computer: WASD to move, the mouse to aim and fire, E to search, Space to dash.
  • On a phone: turn it sideways, use the left thumb to move and the FIRE button to shoot.
  • Crouching in tall grass or bushes keeps guards from spotting you.

Play it now

Pocket Exfil runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
