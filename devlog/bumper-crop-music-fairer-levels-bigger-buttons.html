Bumper Crop gets music, fairer levels and bigger buttons

November 6, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

Bumper Crop is my cozy match-3 farm game: you match crops to bring in the harvest, fill truck orders for coins, and slowly fix up Grandma's run-down farm. This week's update is less about new things to buy and more about how the game feels to play. It has music now, the levels climb in a smoother way, and the buttons are easier to hit with a thumb.

Background music

Until now the farm only had sound effects and farm ambience. I added a gentle background track that plays on every screen. It starts on your first tap, then settles to a much quieter level after the first 45 seconds, so it stays in the background while you think about your next move. It also dips under the big chain reactions, so the harvest sounds still come through.

Plenty of people play with their own music or a podcast on, so on iPhone the track is set up to let your own music app keep playing. If you'd rather have quiet, open the settings button with the two sliders and switch Music off. Sound effects, farm ambience and full animations each have their own switch there too.

Fairer level goals

I retuned the harvest goals on all 45 levels so the difficulty climbs in a steadier way. Now each field starts easy and gets harder toward its tenth level, and each new field starts and ends a little harder than the one before. The West field is still the toughest place on the farm.

I also changed levels 4 and 5 on the Home field. They ask you to break rocks, and you often reach them before you own the chickens, who are the best rock breakers on the farm. They ask for fewer rocks now, so you can beat them without the chickens. The field levels also use all five of their crops from the very first level, so the board looks the same as the levels that come after it.

Five colors of truck

The delivery truck that drives off with your orders now comes in five colors. It's a small thing, but filling orders is something you do a lot, and a little variety makes the farm feel more alive.

A start-over button

There was no easy way to begin a fresh farm on the same device, so the settings screen now has a Start the game over button. It erases your whole farm and starts you from the beginning, tutorial included. Your sound settings stay as they were. It takes two taps, so a stray tap can't erase anything, but there's no undo after the second one.

Bigger tap areas

Some of the smaller buttons, such as the arrows on the level card and the swap button beside each truck order, could be hard to hit on a phone. They look the same as before, but each one now answers to a thumb-sized area around it. Where two areas overlap, the button you actually touched wins, then the nearest one.

What's next

The thing I want most now is to hear how the levels feel to real players. If a level feels unfair, or too easy, tell me which one through the contact page. That kind of note is the most useful thing I get.
