Privacy policy

What this website and our games collect, what they don't, and how ads work here.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Rare Side Effects Studio ("we", "us") makes free browser games. This policy covers this website, sideeffectsstudio.buzz, and our games at their addresses on this domain. Some games also have their own privacy policy with the details for that game (linked below).

The short version

  • You never need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, age or location.
  • Your game progress is saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • We never sell personal information.
  • Nothing in our games costs real money.
  • We may show ads from Google. Google and its partners can use cookies to show those ads. See "Advertising" below.

This website

This website doesn't use accounts, sign-up forms, analytics or tracking cookies of its own. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare runs our domain and the game servers. Like any web host, they automatically see your IP address and browser details when you visit, to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. Our pages load a font from Google Fonts, so Google also sees that request.

Our games

  • Saves: progress is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing your browser's site data erases it.
  • Online features: a few features talk to our own server on Cloudflare, such as the Egg Rush leaderboard in Hatch & Grow and battles against other players in Brainrot Gem Quest. They use a random player ID made by the game, a made-up nickname and your scores or team. They don't use your name or contact details.
  • No chat: there's no chat or messaging between players.

Advertising

We may show ads on this website and in our games to keep them free. Those ads are served by Google (Google AdSense and Google's ads for HTML5 games).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through Google's consent message.

Children

Our games are made for everyone, including kids. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and our games never ask for it. Ads on our sites are set to Google's treatment for child-directed content, which means non-personalized ads only. If you're a parent and think your child has shared personal information with us, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to erase your game saves and any cookies.
  • Use Google's Ads Settings or your browser's settings to block or limit ad cookies.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have leaderboard or battle entries removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
