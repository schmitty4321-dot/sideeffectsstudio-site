How to play

  1. Tap Tronchetto. Every tap fills the energy meter, and a full meter gives you a roll. Taps don't give credits, they only make rolls. Tap fast to fill the frenzy bar: a full bar gives you 10 seconds of double energy.
  2. Spend your rolls on the reels. The reels are 5 wide and 3 tall. Three or more matching symbols in a line pay credits, across, down or along a diagonal. Wins cascade: matched symbols burst, new ones fall in, and each chain step pays more, up to ×5. Tronchetto is the wild.
  3. Open packs with your credits. Each pack holds one card: a new character, an extra copy that raises a character's stars, or an upgrade card. Characters on your team make energy and fill a harvest bank with credits, even while the game is closed.
  4. Grow your camp. As you play, new places open: trips for your benched characters, the lab, the Boss Siege, Patapim's Garden, the Hatchery, the Daily Market and more. A "next goal" chip on the screen always shows what opens next.

Your first 10 minutes

You start with a few free rolls and a short tutorial. Your first goal only takes six taps, so you'll be spinning almost straight away. A free starter crate opens after your first roll, and Day 1 of the daily reward is given to you at once.

The game opens one feature at a time, so the screen never feels crowded:

  • PACKS opens after 3 rolls. Buy a Basic Crate (80 credits) as soon as you can: every character you own earns for you.
  • GOALS opens after 8 rolls. This is where the daily reward, the three daily missions and the trophies live.
  • CAMP opens when you own 7 characters, and the Boss Siege when you own 12.
  • The Training Gym opens at 35 rolls, Patapim's Garden at 65, the roll multiplier at 80 and the Hatchery at 100.

For the first 30 rolls the roll meter costs half the usual energy, so rolls come quickly. Use that time to fill your team. Put your best three characters on the TEAM tab, and remember to come back to the TAP screen: flying characters drop pizza slices, coffee cherries and other goodies, and floating bubbles hold credits, energy and shards. Tap or swipe them to grab them.

At some point you'll run out of rolls. That's normal. The game shows you what you can still do: collect the harvest bank, buy packs, finish missions, hit the Siege boss or simply tap for more energy.

Strategy

Early game: build the team

Your team is your engine. Each character earns energy per second and credits per hour, and rarer characters earn far more: a Common makes about 60 credits an hour at ★1, an Epic about 320 and a Legendary about 700. Characters come in three roles. Harvesters lean toward credits, Tappers lean toward energy, and Bosses also make their own slot symbol pay 10% more per star.

Spend your first credits in this order:

  • Basic Crates until you have a full team of decent characters.
  • Team slots. You start with 3. The 4th costs 1,000 credits, the 5th 6,000 and the 6th 25,000. One more teammate is one more earner, all day long.
  • Roll Power. Each level doubles your bet per roll, so every win doubles too. Level 2 costs 500 credits, level 3 costs 3,000 and level 4 costs 18,000.

Every pack type gets a little pricier each time you buy it, so mixing pack types gives you more cards for your credits. Upgrade cards from Basic Crates are worth keeping: the Golden Bat makes taps stronger, the Espresso Machine makes the roll meter cheaper, the Sahur Alarm lets your team work longer while you're away, and the Harvest Basket adds credits to every harvest.

Reading the reels

  • Sahur meter. Every Tronchetto wild and every losing roll fills it. When it's full, the next roll is a Super Swing with reels 2 and 4 fully wild. So a run of losses is building toward something.
  • Sahur Drums. Three or more anywhere start bonus rolls (8 for three drums, 12 for four or five) in a boss's arena, with that boss's special feature switched on. Lirilì Larilà freezes wilds in place, Brr Brr Patapim makes every wild sprout two more, and La Vaca Saturno starts the win multiplier at ×3 and raises it every roll.
  • Cosmic meter. Every drum also fills this meter. A full meter opens the Cosmic Pick, where you tap planets to match three and win the MINI, MINOR, MAJOR or GRAND jackpot.
  • Sahur Coins. Five or more start Hold & Spin. Coins lock in place, each new coin resets the respins, and filling all 15 spaces wins the GRAND.
  • Bonus Roll Drums. Three, four or five of these bank 2, 4 or 6 extra rolls at the power you won them at.

Middle game: camp, siege and stars

Once CAMP opens, nobody should sit idle. Characters who aren't on your team can go on trips of 1, 4 or 8 hours. Each place leans toward one kind of loot: the Amalfi Beach brings lots of credits, the Clock Tower brings rolls, the Ancient Forest brings higher-tier shards, the Cosmic Farm can bring a free pack, and Venice Canals brings pet eggs. A boss sent to its own arena brings back 50% more. Trips also send postcards home, and postcards can hold seeds and eggs you can't buy.

The Boss Siege is the only way to win boss characters: packs never contain them. One boss camps in town at a time. Your team wears it down every second, even while you're away, and your taps on the Siege screen add damage. While you watch, the boss fights back, so tap it when it attacks or it heals. Every other boss on your team adds 25% siege damage. After a win, the next boss arrives 8 hours later.

Use the lab without a break: it runs one project at a time. Two projects change how the game feels. The Harvest Manager collects your harvest bank for you, and the Roll Manager plays your saved rolls while you're away. Expedition Tents let you run more trips at once.

Raise stars with spare copies. A character needs 2 copies for ★2 and 8 for ★5, and a ★5 character earns three times as much as at ★1. The Training Gym sells one copy toward the next star when luck isn't on your side. Shards merge four into one of the next tier, and enough shards craft any character you're missing.

Late game: Brainrot cards, the Black Market and new towns

The rarest cards, the two Brainrot characters, only come from Brainrot Boxes (3,500 credits to start). You're guaranteed one at least every 50 Brainrot Boxes, and any pack type guarantees a Legendary or better at least every 30 packs.

The Black Market dealer peeks in during the last 5 minutes of every hour. He trades spare cards for a card of the next tier: 5 Commons for a Rare, 5 Rares for an Epic, or 8 Epics for a Legendary. You get 2 trades a day, and about one trade in six is a "bootleg": you still get the real card, with a silly sticker on its face.

When you've earned enough credits in one run, the New Town option opens. Travelling starts a new run in the next Italian town: credits, Roll Power, team slots, research and landmarks reset, but you keep your whole collection, stars, shards and upgrade cards. You earn Moons for the credits you made, and every Moon ever earned adds 2% energy and credits for good. Spend Moons on permanent perks such as Harvest Moon (more team credits), Head Start (start each town at a higher Roll Power) and Bargain Moon (cheaper packs). Each town also has one exclusive Legendary character. A good time to travel is when progress in your current town slows to a crawl.

Tips

  • Come back every day: the 7-day streak gives rolls, credits and two Boss Boxes, and three finished missions add a free crate.
  • Your team's bank only fills for 8 hours while you're away at first. Check in before bed and after waking up.
  • Tronchetto only gets tired after 15 minutes of play. Grab the Pocket Watch drop to shake off tiredness at once.
  • Watch for random events: Espresso Hour doubles your team for 10 minutes, Lucky Night adds 50% to roll wins, and in a Bombardiro Raid you tap the falling bombs for credits.
  • Tapping tools from the Customize shop only work while Tronchetto wears his classic look.
  • Pick one active pet in the Hatchery. It bounces around Tronchetto's feet, grabs nearby drops and adds a little energy.
  • Nothing in the game costs real money, ever.

For parents

  • Cost: free to play. There are no real-money purchases of any kind. Credits, rolls and packs are only earned by playing, and nothing pays out.
  • The reels: the game has a bonus slot machine, but it uses free in-game credits only. You can't put money in or take money out.
  • Ads: the game itself shows no ads.
  • Chat and online play: none. There's no way to talk to or see other players.
  • Data: no accounts and no sign-in. The game never asks for a name, email, birthday, photo or location. Progress is saved only in your browser on your device, and the game sends nothing to us.
  • Age: cartoon characters, bright colours and silly humour. Fights are slapstick (a bat, cartoon bombs), with no blood. It suits children who can read short labels, and plenty of adults enjoy it too.

FAQ

Does Tap Tap Sahur cost money?

No. It's free, and there's nothing to buy. Every credit, roll and pack is earned in the game.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser's storage on the device you play on. It saves by itself as you play. Clearing your browser data, or playing in a private window, will lose it.

How do I move my game to another device?

Open Settings (the gear, top right) and make a save code. Copy it, then paste it into Settings on the new device. Keep the code somewhere safe as a backup too.

I lost my progress. What happened?

Saves belong to one browser at one web address. A different browser, a private window or cleared site data starts a fresh game. If you made a save code, you can restore it from Settings.

Can I play on iPhone or iPad?

Yes. Open the game in Safari. You can also use Share, then Add to Home Screen, to get an app icon.

Can I play offline?

After you've played once online, the game can usually start without a connection, because your browser keeps a copy. Updates arrive the next time you play online.

Why do my taps feel weaker?

After 15 minutes of play Tronchetto starts to tire, so each tap gives less energy. He recovers over time, and the Pocket Watch drop and the Power Naps research help.

How do I get boss characters?

Only from the Boss Siege in CAMP. Packs never contain bosses. Beat the boss camping in town to claim its card.
