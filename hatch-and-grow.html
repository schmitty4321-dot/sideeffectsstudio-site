Hatch & Grow

A cozy merge game: hatch eggs, merge matching characters to grow them up from Baby to Legend, and fill orders for the neighbours on Sahur Street.

How to play

  1. Hatch. Tap an egg in the tray and a baby character pops onto the board.
  2. Merge. Drag one character onto a matching one. The pair merges into the next stage: Baby, Kid, Teen, Adult, then Legend.
  3. Fill orders. The neighbours on Sahur Street ask for characters at certain stages. Deliver them for coins, and clear the rubble to unlock more of the board.
  4. Grow your street. Buy more eggs, earn coins while you're away, send grown-ups on trips that mail home postcards, defend the street from each zone's boss and race the week's Egg Rush leaderboard.

Tips

  • Keep a free spot or two on the board so you always have room to merge.
  • Orders pay best for older stages, so let a few characters grow up.
  • Your street keeps earning while the game is closed.

Play it now

Hatch & Grow runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
