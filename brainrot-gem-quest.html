How to play

  1. Mine. Bounce the ball to smash walls of gems. Exploding, lightning and multiball bricks clear big chunks at once.
  2. Refine. Take your haul to the bubble shooter and match colours to refine gems, before the field creeps down and crushes you.
  3. Battle. Fight the boss of each stop: parry its attacks, swipe to strike and chain combos.
  4. Catch and collect. Seal the capture ring to add the boss to your crew, open packs for new characters, travel the world tour and brawl other players' teams.

Tips

  • Power bricks are the fastest way through a tough wall.
  • Claim your daily reward each day for free packs and gems.
  • A bigger crew makes the later bosses much easier.
