About Rare Side Effects

A one-person game studio run by a doctor who makes small, loud, free games.

Hi, I'm Dr.Dev

I'm a doctor by day and a game developer the rest of the time. Rare Side Effects Studio is my one-person studio: I design, build and test every game here.

I make the kind of games I'd want on a short break: quick to pick up, hard to put down, and silly on purpose. They all play right in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to download or install.

What we promise

  • Free to play. Every game is free, start to finish.
  • No real-money purchases. Nothing in our games can be bought with real money. Any in-game currency is earned by playing.
  • No accounts. You never need to sign up or give us your name or email.
  • No chat with strangers. There's no messaging between players.
  • Ads, kept fair. To keep the games free we may show ads. We prefer optional ones, like "watch an ad for a bonus", over ads that interrupt you.

Why "Rare Side Effects"?

Every medicine comes with a label of possible side effects, most of them rare. Ours include uncontrollable tapping, yelling "LEGENDARY" at 2 a.m. and naming houseplants after bosses. It's a joke, not medical advice.
