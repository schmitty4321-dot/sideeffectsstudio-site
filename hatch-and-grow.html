Hatch & Grow

A cozy merge game: hatch eggs, merge matching characters to grow them up from Baby to Legend, and fill orders for the neighbours on Sahur Street.

How to play

  1. Hatch. Tap an egg in your tray. A character lands on the first free square of the board, usually as a Baby. A new free egg arrives every few seconds, and you can buy more with coins.
  2. Merge. Drag a character onto the same character at the same stage. They stack, and the badge shows how close the stack is (2/3 means one more to go). A full stack grows into the next stage: 2 Babies make a Kid, 2 Kids make a Teen, 3 Teens make an Adult and 5 Adults make a Legend.
  3. Earn. Everything on the board earns coins every second, and older stages earn far more. A Baby earns 1 coin a second and a Legend earns 160. The board keeps earning while the game is closed, for up to 2 hours.
  4. Fill orders. Customers like Nonna Pina and Chef Luigi ask for characters at certain stages. Drag the right one onto their card. When the card is full, collect the reward.
  5. Finish the zone. Each zone has two characters. Grow a Legend of the first one to unlock the second, then grow a Legend of the second to complete the zone. Beat the zone's boss to travel on to the next zone.

Your first 10 minutes

You start in Zone 1, Sahur Street, with three Log eggs in the tray and a 20-square board. A gold ring and a little animated hand walk you through the first moves: tap an egg, tap one more, then drag one character onto its twin. After that the game only shows a hint the first time something new matters, so you're never stuck reading a wall of text.

Your first character is Tronchetto. Hatch every egg you have, then merge every matching pair you can see. A Kid earns more than the two Babies that made it, so watch your coins-per-second number climb as you merge.

The pile of rocks on one square is rubble. Tap it 25 times to break it for coins and free the square. Sometimes rubble hides Tickets or a card pack.

Next, look at the Orders. The first order is set up to ask for a character you already have on the board, so you can drag it over straight away. Orders pay well, often thousands of coins in Zone 1, which is a lot when your first eggs cost only tens of coins.

The first few minutes of every zone are a gentle start: eggs are much cheaper and free eggs come faster. Buy eggs whenever you can and keep merging. Your first Legend Tronchetto unlocks Sharkolo, the zone's second character, who earns ten times as much.

Strategy

Early game: get your income going

  • Merge before you buy. Coins come from what's on the board, and older stages earn much more per square. A Kid earns 2.2 coins a second, a Teen 4.8, an Adult 15 and a Legend 160. One Legend out-earns a board full of Babies.
  • Hatch eggs quickly. An egg left in the tray too long starts to crack (after 60 seconds in Zone 1, and sooner in later zones). Leave it twice as long and it turns green and hatches a plain Baby, even from an egg that could have hatched a Kid or a Teen.
  • Know your eggs. A Log egg (the first character's egg) hatches a Baby most of the time, a Kid about 1 time in 6 and now and then a Teen. Gold eggs always hatch a Kid or a Teen. Mystery eggs hatch a Teen, an Adult or, rarely, a Legend, or they burst into a bag of coins.
  • Egg prices go up as you buy, but an egg never costs more than about 15 seconds of your income, so eggs stay affordable as you grow.

Middle game: orders, the second character and Legend powers

  • Keep the Orders moving. You have three order cards. When you collect one, a new customer arrives about 20 seconds later. Skipping an order makes you wait a minute for the next one, so only skip orders you really can't fill.
  • Rush orders pay triple. After your first delivery, some orders come with a countdown. They pay three times as much, but the customer leaves when the clock runs out. Fill these first.
  • Bigger orders, bigger extras. Orders that ask for a Teen or older can also give a Gold egg, a Mystery egg or a card pack.
  • Once the second character is unlocked, more orders ask for it, and some of your free eggs (about 1 in 8) arrive as its eggs. It earns ten times as much as the first character at every stage, so it quickly becomes your main earner.
  • Legend powers. Keep a Legend on the board to switch on its power. The first character's Legend makes free eggs arrive 20% faster (Drum Call). The second character's Legend merges two matching Babies for you every 30 seconds (Feeding Frenzy). With both Legends on the board at once, they lay a free Mystery egg every 10 minutes (Mystery Nest). You can see which powers are on under Home, Legend powers.
  • Merge in quick bursts. Merges made within 3 seconds of each other build a combo, and every merge in the chain pays bonus coins, with the bonus growing up to ×8. Line up several pairs and merge them one after another.
  • Chase off the Sneaky Salami. While you play, a thief sometimes creeps toward your egg tray. Every merge pushes him back, and tapping him bonks him. Chase him off and he drops coins. Let him reach the tray and he steals an egg.

Late game: bosses, Tickets and the zones beyond

  • Boss battles are lane defense. When the zone is complete, its boss blocks the road. The boss throws clutter down three lanes toward your egg nest. Tap a fighter card, then tap a lane to station it there (2 per lane). Fighters smash clutter but tire and need a rest, so tap clutter yourself to help. Five pieces getting through ends the battle. Your strongest characters on the board become your team, and Legends are the strongest, so fight with Legends on the board.
  • Winning pays Tickets (100 for Zone 1, and 20 more for each zone after that), two new comic pages, a free card pack and a big pile of coins. Then you can travel on.
  • Spend Tickets in the Store. The Store (in Home) sells the five egg upgrades, which stay with you in every zone: Hatch all (10 Tickets, one button hatches the whole tray), Bigger tray (30, holds 8 eggs instead of 5), Turbo eggs (40, free eggs come twice as fast), Twin eggs (60, 1 in 4 free eggs arrives as two) and Legend nest (80, each Legend on the board makes free eggs 5% faster, up to 50%). Buy Hatch all and Bigger tray first. Card packs cost 25 Tickets.
  • Travelling is a fresh start. The new zone has a new board, new eggs, new customers and two new characters, and your coins start again from zero. Everything in the new zone earns 8 times as much (and costs 8 times as much) as the zone before. Your cards, upgrades, Tickets and comic pages come with you.
  • The ten zones are Sahur Street, Bakery, Pizzeria, Beach, Forest, Spooky, Gym, Volcano, Sky and Legends. Later customers are pickier, and the last boss, Re Ombra, uses every boss power.
  • Collect cards. Every new card in your album adds a little income, and each finished album page adds more. Duplicates level a card up.
  • Send cards on Trips. From Home, send a card on a 1, 4 or 8 hour trip to places like Amalfi Beach (lots of coins) or the Venice Canals (a good chance of a Mystery egg). Travellers often mail a postcard home with a small gift, and stronger cards bring back more. A third trip slot opens once you own 8 cards.

Every day: Home and Egg Rush

The Home button at the top opens daily rewards, the daily spin, the card album, the Store, Trips, the comic and Egg Rush. Play on following days to build a login streak (Day 7 pays two card packs and 25 Tickets). The 3 daily missions pay coins and Tickets, and finishing all three adds a free card pack. The daily spin is free once a day.

Egg Rush is an endless arcade mini-game on a small 5×5 board. Eggs drop faster and faster. Tap an egg to hatch it and drag two of the same character together to grow them up. Two Legends together go boom for big points, and quick merges build a combo. The run ends when an egg has nowhere to land. Your score turns into coins, and you get a free card pack the first time you pass 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 points. The weekly board starts fresh every Monday.

Tips

  • Keep a free square or two on the board so you always have room to hatch and merge.
  • Board full? Merge, or drag a character to the bin to sell it for coins.
  • Clear rubble early: the square is worth more than the taps.
  • Keep at least one Legend of each character on the board for the Legend powers.
  • Your board earns for up to 2 hours while you're away, so check in a few times a day.
  • The "?" button at the top shows the rules again any time.

For parents

  • Free, with no real-money purchases. Everything in the game, including card packs and upgrades, is bought with coins or Tickets that you earn by playing. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • No ads. The game doesn't show any advertising.
  • No chat. There's no way to message other players, and no place to type anything.
  • Where progress is saved: in the browser on your own device. Nobody needs an account, an email address or a name.
  • The one online feature is the Egg Rush leaderboard. When you open it, the game creates an anonymous leaderboard profile with a random ID and a made-up name like "Speedy Bagelino 482", picked by the game from a fixed list. After each Egg Rush run it sends the score and how long the run lasted. Other players see only the made-up name, the best score and the rank. The game never asks for or sends a real name, email, location or photo.
  • Age suitability: it's a gentle, cartoon game with silly food-and-animal characters. Boss battles are about tapping away flying clutter, nobody gets hurt, and losing costs nothing. It suits young children who can drag and tap, and the reading is short and simple.

FAQ

Does Hatch & Grow cost money?

No. It's free to play, and there's nothing to buy with real money. Tickets, card packs and upgrades are all earned in the game.

I lost my progress. Where did it go?

Your save lives in the browser on the device you played on. Clearing your browser's data for the site, using a private window, or playing on a different browser or device starts a new game. If you played at a different web address before, that progress stays at that address.

Can I play on an iPhone, iPad or Android phone?

Yes. It runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers, upright or sideways. There's nothing to install.

Can I play offline?

You need a connection to load the game. The Egg Rush world leaderboard also needs a connection. If it can't be reached, your runs are still kept under "My best runs" on your device.

How do I unlock the second character?

Grow a Legend of the zone's first character. That unlocks the second character, puts one of its eggs in your tray and adds its Buy button. Growing a Legend of the second character completes the zone.

Why did my egg hatch a plain Baby?

Eggs that sit in the tray too long crack, then turn green. A green egg always hatches a Baby. Hatch your eggs soon after they arrive.

Who is the Sneaky Salami?

A thief who sometimes sneaks toward your egg tray while you play. Merge characters or tap him to push him back. If you chase him off he drops coins. If he reaches the tray he takes an egg.

What are Tickets for?

Tickets are spent in the Store on egg upgrades and card packs. You get them from boss wins, daily missions, the daily spin, the 7-day login streak and, now and then, from breaking rubble.

I lost a boss battle. Did I lose anything?

No. Your board is safe. Grow more or older characters (Legends are strongest) and try again whenever you like.

Play it now

Hatch & Grow runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
