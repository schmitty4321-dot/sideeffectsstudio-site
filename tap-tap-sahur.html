How to play

  1. Tap Tronchetto. Every tap fills the energy meter, and a full meter gives you a roll. Keep tapping fast for a Tap Frenzy.
  2. Spend your rolls on the reels. Matching symbols pay credits, wins cascade into more wins, and giant wilds can cover whole reels. Five drums start a bonus round.
  3. Open packs with your credits. Each pack adds characters to your crew, and your crew keeps earning credits even while the game is closed.
  4. Take on the bosses and the extras. Beat boss bonus rounds, send your crew on camp trips, try the lab, read the story chapters and follow the Grand Journey for milestone rewards.

Tips

  • Come back every day: your crew's earnings pile up while you're away.
  • Rare characters are worth more, so keep opening packs to fill the collection.
  • Nothing in the game costs real money, ever.
