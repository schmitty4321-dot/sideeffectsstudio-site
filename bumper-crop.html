Bumper Crop

How to play

Bumper Crop has two halves. On the field you play match-3 levels to bring in crops. On the farm you spend those crops and the coins they earn to fix up Grandma's old place.

  1. Tap the field to start a level. The card shows the harvest you need, for example 35 wheat, and how many moves you have.
  2. Swap two neighboring crops to line up three or more of a kind. Matched crops are harvested and count toward the goal at the top. Every swap uses one move.
  3. Let crops ripen. A crop left on the field grows each move. A sprout pays 1, a ripe crop pays 2 and a golden crop pays 3.
  4. Make power-ups. Four in a line makes a tractor that clears a whole row or column. An L or T shape makes a sprinkler that soaks the 3×3 patch around it. Five in a line makes a golden seed that harvests every crop of the kind you swap it with. Tap a power-up to fire it, or swap two together for a bigger blast.
  5. Win the level by reaching every goal before the moves run out. Moves you save become a harvest party, where your animals run across the field and pick extra crops.
  6. Sell to the delivery truck. Back on the farm, tap the truck to see three orders. Deliver one when you have everything it asks for, and the coins are yours.
  7. Spend coins on the farm. Buy Daisy's pasture, animals, new fields and buildings, then open the Empire map to see everything you can own.

Your first 10 minutes

Mrs. Hazel, a neighbor, meets you at the gate and walks you through the basics. She shows you how to drag a wheat into a line of three, how the goal counter works, and how four in a row earns a tractor. You only need to follow the glowing spot she points at, and you can skip her tips at any time.

After level 1 she takes you to the truck. The first orders are small and the same for everyone: 8 wheat, then 6 wheat and 6 carrots. Deliver them for your first coins.

Your first big goal is the cow pasture for 100 coins. Buying it brings Daisy the cow home. Tap her to feed her 5 wheat. Her first milk is ready in about two minutes, so play another level while you wait. When she's ready, tap her again to play Daisy's Milk Run: slide the crate left and right to catch milk bottles, grab the golden ones and dodge the mud. Fill the crate past the first mark for 2 bottles and past the second for 3. Milk sells for a lot on the truck, and each milking also gives you a fertilizer.

Hazel's last stop is the Empire map. From there you can see every field, animal and building in the game. If you ever wonder what to do next, look for the gold glow on the farm and the hint line on the farm screen. They always point at the most useful thing to do.

Strategy

Early game: the Home field (levels 1 to 5)

The Home field has wheat, carrots, corn and tomatoes, and pumpkins join from level 3. Crows start showing up on level 2 and the weather from level 3. Levels 4 and 5 add rocks that you need to break, and gophers.

  • Buy in this order: the cow pasture (100 coins), then the dog house (200) and the chicken coop (300). Biscuit the dog and the chickens are your first level helpers, and each one makes every later level easier.
  • Know your helpers. Each animal has its own meter under the board. Daisy's meter fills from crops you harvest, and she stomps a 3×3 patch. Biscuit's fills as you make moves, and he herds every crop of one kind into the middle column so they line up. The chickens' meter fills from breaking rocks and chasing pests, and they peck every rock and weed on the field. A full meter glows, and using it costs no move.
  • Read the forecast. The weather sign tells you what's coming and in how many moves. Rain ripens everything. Sun doubles the harvest on one row for two moves, so match on that row. Wind slides one column over, and any match it makes is a free harvest.
  • Chase pests fast. A crow lands on your ripest crop. Tap it to chase it off and earn a crop. Leave it, and that crop goes back to a sprout. Gophers don't wait as long, but a bonk pays 3 crops. Miss one and the crops around it turn back into sprouts.
  • Aim for stars. You get 3 stars when you win with at least 40% of your moves left, and 2 stars with at least 20%. Each new star pays coins and one gem, so it's worth replaying an old level to improve it. Use the arrows on the level card to pick any level you've reached.

Middle game: the North and East fields (levels 6 to 25)

Level 6 and up need the North field (500 coins, and you must have milked Daisy once). Each field you buy opens 10 new levels with a star crop, a new crop and its own trouble. On the North field weeds creep onto the crops next to them, so clear them early. The East field (900) brings mud that sticks crops in place. A match right next to the mud washes it off. Later fields pay more coins per star.

  • Let your fields work for you. Bought fields grow crops on their own, even while you're away, until they're full. Collect them from the Empire map. The Tractor (600) makes them grow twice as fast and the Silo (800) lets them hold twice as much.
  • Cook before you sell. The Kitchen (450) turns crops into food that sells for more. Bread takes 20 wheat and tomato soup takes 20 tomatoes. It also bakes two treats for your levels: carrot cake starts every helper with a full meter and one free refill, and pumpkin cookies give 3 extra moves.
  • Use the level extras. Before you press Start, the level card lets you switch on extras. A fertilizer starts the level with a sprinkler on the field. An animal snack costs 10 carrots and fills every helper meter, so they can help from your first move.
  • Level up your pets. Feed a pet 10 of its favorite food to give it experience. Daisy likes carrots, Biscuit likes wheat and the chickens like corn. Higher levels refill the meter partly after each use, and at level 3 Daisy stomps a bigger patch, Biscuit herds two lines and the chickens peck twice. From level 2 each pet brings you a gift once a day.
  • Open the farm stand. For 350 coins, neighbors stop by and ask for a few crops. They pay coins plus seeds, a yard decoration or a gem, and every favor counts toward rebuilding the town: first the old bridge, then the town square, then the library.
  • Grow more animals. The sheep pen (700) makes wool from corn and the pig pen (1,100) finds truffles with pumpkins. Feed an animal its favorite crop to fill its hearts. Five hearts bring a baby, up to three per kind, and grown babies work too. Each grown calf adds an extra bottle of milk.

Late game: the South and West fields (levels 26 to 45)

The South field (1,500) has vines that spread every move you don't cut one, so a match beside a vine should come first. The West field (2,400) is the hardest: frost can freeze a whole row for 3 moves, and it adds rocks, mud and gophers. Watch for the frost on the forecast and make your big matches before it lands.

  • Save gems for the big three. Gems come from new stars, every 5th truck delivery, fair ribbons and gifts. The Harvester (1,000 coins and 10 gems) collects your fields for you. Restoring the farmhouse costs 3,000 coins and 15 gems.
  • Restore the rooms. After the farmhouse, five rooms open one by one, from the kitchen to Grandma's study. Each room holds one of Grandma's letters and adds 3% to everything the truck pays.
  • Upgrade. Fields, the tractor, the silo and the kitchen can each be upgraded to level 5. Every level costs a lot more than the last, and the final one also costs 5 gems.
  • Enter the county fair. For 400 coins you can grow a giant pumpkin. Feed it 10 pumpkins up to three times a day. Enter it at size 6 for a bronze ribbon, 12 for silver or 21 for gold, which pays 600 coins and 12 gems.
  • Fly the bees. The bee hive (1,200) opens Pollen Run, a 20-second game where you guide a bee to flowers and dodge webs and rain. It makes 1 to 3 honey, and the bees give every bought field 5 minutes of growth. You can fly again every 15 minutes.

Tips

  • A 2×2 square of the same crop is a match too, and it sends a bee to pick one of the crops your goal still needs.
  • Harvesting 12 or more crops in one move earns a golden basket with 3 extra crops.
  • Two golden seeds swapped together harvest the whole board.
  • Don't rush. Waiting a couple of moves lets crops ripen to double or triple value, but keep an eye on crows.
  • Can't fill an order? Tap the ↻ button beside it for a new one. You can swap once every 30 seconds.
  • Once you own a helper, the Farm friends button calls piglets, ducks, a goat and others. Each one uses a full meter. The goat eats every weed on the field.
  • The Barn cat (900) chases pests off for you, and Maple the pony (1,400) hauls extra crops home after every level.

For parents

  • Free, with no purchases. Bumper Crop has no real-money purchases. Coins and gems are earned only by playing, and gems can't be bought.
  • Ads. The game shows no ads right now. When a level runs out of moves, a player can take 5 more moves once per level after a short pause.
  • Where progress is saved. The farm is saved in the browser on the device you play on. Clearing the browser's site data, or playing in a private window, will lose it. There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Online features. There are none. The game has no chat, no friend lists, no leaderboards and no messages from other players, and it doesn't send your child's play or personal details anywhere. It needs the internet only to load the game and its music.
  • Age suitability. It's a gentle farm game with no violence and no scary moments. The worst thing that happens is a crow pecking a carrot. Short reading helps with the tutorial and Grandma's letters, so younger children may like a grown-up nearby at first.
  • Time. Some things run on real-time timers, such as Daisy's milk and the giant pumpkin's daily feedings, so the game rewards short visits more than long sessions.

FAQ

Does Bumper Crop cost money?

No. It's free to play in your browser and has no real-money purchases of any kind.

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

Your farm is saved only in your browser on that device. If the browser's data was cleared, or you played in a private window, the save is gone and can't be recovered. A different browser or device starts its own new farm.

Can I play on an iPhone or iPad?

Yes. Open the game in Safari or any other modern browser. It plays best with the phone held upright.

Can I play offline?

You need an internet connection to open the game. There's nothing to install.

Why can't I start the next level?

Levels 6 to 45 belong to the North, East, South and West fields. If the level card tells you to buy a field first, buy that field on the Empire map. The North field also needs you to milk Daisy once.

What happens if I run out of moves?

You keep every crop you harvested. You can take 5 more moves once per level, but a level won that way only earns one star. You can replay it any time for more.

What are gems for?

Gems pay for the biggest things on the farm: the Harvester, the farmhouse, its rooms, the last upgrade level, rare seeds, and fancy outfits in the pet shop.

What do seeds do?

A seed changes what one of your bought fields grows, so you can grow the crop you need most. You start with 3 and get more from neighbors and pet gifts.

How do I turn the sound or music off?

Tap the settings button with the two sliders. You can switch sound effects, music, farm sounds and full animations on or off there. The same screen has a button to start the whole game over.

Play it now

Bumper Crop runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
