How to play

Swap two neighboring crops to line up three or more of a kind and harvest them. Each level asks for a set harvest before your moves run out. Crops left on the field ripen and pay more. Sell what you grow to the delivery truck for coins, then spend your coins on fields, animals and repairs around the farm. Each new field brings its own crops and its own trouble, from creeping weeds to frost.

Tips

  • Four in a row earns a tractor, an L or T shape a sprinkler, and five in a row a golden seed.
  • Your animals have meters that fill as you play. A full one glows, and using it costs no move.
  • Stuck on what to do next? Look for the gold glow on the farm.

Play it now

Bumper Crop runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
