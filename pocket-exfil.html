Pocket Exfil

A top-down extraction shooter with chunky toy soldiers: drop into a raid, sneak or fight past guard squads, grab what you can and reach an exit before time runs out.

How to play

  1. Gear up in the hideout. Pick your camo team with the badges at the top (each team has its own ability), put a gun in your loadout, add meds or grenades to your backpack, and choose a map. Then press Deploy.
  2. Ride in. A jeep or a helicopter drops you at the edge of the map. Each raid has a 5-minute timer and two exits, marked EXFIL. Arrows at the edge of the screen point to them.
  3. Loot. Sneak through tall grass and bushes, or fight guard squads head on. Search crates, lockers, jeep trunks and supply drops, and put what you find in your backpack.
  4. Get out. Stand inside a green EXFIL until its countdown ends. Everything you carry comes home to your stash. If you go down, or the timer runs out, you lose your backpack and the gear you wore, but your secure pouch is always kept.
  5. Grow stronger. Sell loot, buy better guns and gear, take contracts, finish challenges, level up and spend perk points, then take on each map's boss.

Controls

  • On a computer: WASD to move, the mouse to aim, click (or hold) to fire, R to reload, Space to dash, E to search, pick up and use, Tab for your backpack, Q to heal, C to crouch, V for the knife, G to throw a grenade (Z picks which kind) and F for your team ability.
  • On a phone: turn it sideways. Your left thumb moves you, and a hard flick dashes. Hold the FIRE button to shoot, and drag it to aim while you fire. The USE button appears when there's something to search or pick up. The other buttons at the screen edges are your backpack, heal, dash, crouch, grenade, ability, reload and knife.

Your first 10 minutes

The first time you open the game, it offers a training raid of about 2 minutes on the Training Yard. Take it. It walks you through six steps, one at a time: move to a marker, search a crate, pick up the loot, shoot a target dummy, crouch behind sandbags, and extract. Nothing can hurt you there. If you skip it, you can replay it later from the gear (⚙) menu.

Back in the hideout you start with $500, a pistol and a bandage. The pistol and bandage are a free kit: if you ever come home with no gun or no meds, the Free Kit button gives you a new set. Kit items sell for $0, so they're only there to get you back into a raid.

Your first raid is on The Compound, a farm and a depot with fields and woods to sneak through. Don't try to clear the map. Instead:

  1. Look for the two EXFIL arrows as soon as you land, so you know where you're heading.
  2. Move from cover to cover. Guards stand at posts and look in one direction, and a yellow cone shows where an unalerted guard is looking. Crouch in tall grass or a bush and they only notice you up close.
  3. Search the crates in quiet buildings. Searching takes a moment and stops if you move, fire or get hit.
  4. Once your bag has a few things in it, head for an exit and get out. A small haul that comes home is worth more than a big one that doesn't.

The line under HIDEOUT always shows your next goal, like "make it out alive" or "unlock Sarge's". Follow it and you'll learn the game in the right order.

Strategy

Early game: get home with something

  • Use the secure pouch. It holds two small items and you keep it even when you go down. Put your most valuable small find in it.
  • Pick the right exit. Green exits are open. Amber exits need something first: a cash fare, a lever that sounds the alarm and opens the gate after a delay, or a Locker Key. Fares come out of your cash, not your backpack.
  • Stay hidden for a bonus. If you extract without ever being spotted or hit, you get a ghost bonus of $300 and 150 XP. Knife takedowns on guards who haven't noticed you are silent. But guards who find a body go looking.
  • Watch for the alarm. When a guard spots you, a squad mate may run to the nearest siren. If he reaches it, every guard hunts you and backup arrives. Take down the runner, or sabotage a siren with E before it's used.
  • Don't overfill your bag. Over three quarters full, you move slower and dash less often. Completely full, you make noise when you move fast.

Middle game: Sarge, contracts and better gear

  • Sarge's shop opens after 5 extracts. He sells gear, mods, bigger backpacks and better guns, and buys your loot for more than the basic Sell box. Spend money with him to raise his rank and unlock more stock. He also posts Wanted items you can turn in for extra cash.
  • Wear a vest and helmet. A vest cuts the damage from every hit. A helmet blocks the first burst that hits you each raid. Both are lost if you go down, so insure them: tap the shield badge on the slot. Insurance costs 15% of the item's value at deploy, and a lost insured item usually comes back after your next raid.
  • Take a contract. The Jobs board offers three: grab a case from a building, take down a marked guard, blow up a radio box, or scout from lookout points. Contracts pay cash and XP, but only if you extract.
  • Mod your gun. Drop a mod on the gun in your loadout and it stays attached. A scope adds bullet speed and damage, a suppressor keeps your shots quiet, a laser or red dot tightens your aim, and bigger magazines mean fewer reloads. Found guns roll a quality, from Worn up to Elite.
  • Check the market and the next raid. Each day one kind of loot sells for ×1.5 and one for ×0.8, shown above the trader. The Next Raid strip shows the twist for your next raid: rain, fog, payday (more loot), lockdown (a harsher alarm) or a short fuse (4 minutes, 3 exits).
  • Use the bench. Scrap junk into parts and craft useful items from them.

Late game: perks, bosses and the big maps

  • Spend perk points. You get a point each level and a bonus point every 5th level, up to level 50. There are four trees: Assault (reloads, recoil, magazines), Survival (health, meds, vest), Ghost (staying unseen) and Scavenger (faster searching, better selling, contracts). Some tiers make you choose one of two perks. Resetting your perks is always free, so try different builds. Guards get a little tougher as you level up.
  • Hunt the bosses. After 3 extracts, raids can turn into boss raids, and the Next Raid strip names the boss. Each map has its own: Colonel Ironjaw (The Compound), Major Velvet (Main Street), Captain Coal (Rail Yard), Admiral Reed (Riverside) and Warden Frostbyte (Snow Outpost). Each boss camps with a lieutenant and two guards at the post farthest from where you land, and a boss bar appears when you get close. Bosses drop their signature item and their own gun. Picking those up makes you "hot": for a while, nearby guards keep coming to look for you.
  • Grab the supply drop. With 2:30 left, a plane drops a crate. It holds great loot, but guards head for it too, and opening it takes 8 seconds and makes you hot.
  • Learn the maps. Main Street has shops, alleys and walk-on roofs. Rail Yard has boxcars, warehouses and a freight train exit you have to catch. Riverside is split by a river, so pick your crossing. On Snow Outpost guards can follow your footprints in the snow, and blizzards cut how far they can see.
  • Don't give up when you go down. Once a raid you get a second wind: for 8 seconds you can crawl, and guards can't see you. Reach an exit and finish its countdown to still get out.

Team abilities

  • Timber: places a sandbag wall for cover (up to 2).
  • Dune: marks every guard nearby for 6 seconds, even through roofs.
  • Concrete: a burst of extra speed and faster firing.
  • Frost: drops a trap that slows guards near it.
  • Shade: cloaks you for 4 seconds, until you fire.

Tips

  • Crouch in tall grass or bushes and guards only see you up close.
  • Crouching behind low cover blocks bullets. Standing behind it only halves them.
  • Climb a tower or a roof to see over walls, but guards up there see farther too.
  • Smoke grenades block guards' view. Flashbangs stun them. Frag grenades can hurt you too.
  • Explosive barrels chain together. Shoot one when guards stand near it.
  • A guard with a key icon drops a Locker Key. Lockers hold a good gun.
  • Mailboxes on the map let you post your most valuable item home for a 10% fee, even if you go down later.
  • On a phone, the gear (⚙) menu lets you change the view size to see more of the map.

For parents

  • Free, with no real-money purchases. All the money in the game is play money earned in raids. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • No ads. The game doesn't show any advertising.
  • No chat and no online play. Pocket Exfil is single-player. There are no other players, no chat and no leaderboard, and the game doesn't send anything about the player anywhere.
  • Where progress is saved: in the browser on your own device. No account, email or name is needed. A save button in the hideout can copy the save as a text code, for a backup or to move it to another device.
  • About the shooting: this is a shooting game, and the player shoots at enemy soldiers and gets shot at. It's drawn in a cartoon style with chunky, toy-like soldiers and guns, seen from above. There's no blood or gore: a guard who goes down flops over, vanishes in a puff and leaves a loot bag behind. There's also a knife move and grenades. It plays fast and can be tense when the timer runs low.
  • Age suitability: the cartoon look keeps it mild, but the whole game is about combat between soldiers. We think it suits older children and up, and parents of younger kids may want to watch a raid first and decide.

FAQ

Does Pocket Exfil cost money?

No. It's free, and there's nothing to buy with real money. All the cash in the game is earned by selling loot.

I lost my progress. Where did it go?

Your save lives in the browser on the device you played on. Clearing your browser's data for the site, using a private window, or switching browser or device starts fresh. Use the save button in the hideout to copy your save as a text code, and import it again if you need to.

Can I play on an iPhone or Android phone?

Yes, in the web browser. Turn the phone sideways: the game asks you to if you hold it upright. The touch controls are made for two thumbs.

Can I play offline?

You need a connection to load the game. The game itself has no online features.

What do I lose when I go down?

Your backpack, your gun, your vest and your helmet. Your secure pouch and everything in your stash are safe. Insured gear usually comes back after your next raid.

Why can't I use an amber exit?

Amber exits are locked. They need a cash fare, a lever that opens the gate after a delay (and sounds the alarm), or a key. The label under the exit tells you which.

How do I unlock Sarge's shop?

Extract successfully 5 times. The button by your secure pouch shows how many you have.

How do I change maps?

Tap the map name at the top of the hideout to switch to the next map. There are five: The Compound, Main Street, Rail Yard, Riverside and Snow Outpost.

Can I play the training again?

Yes. Open the gear (⚙) menu and choose Replay training.

Is there blood?

No. Downed soldiers fall over and turn into loot bags. There's no blood or gore anywhere in the game.

Play it now

Pocket Exfil runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
