The Grand Journey, Cave-In and new minigames

October 23, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

Brainrot Gem Quest has a lot going on: a mine, a bubble refinery, a forge, boss fights, a crew, Brawl and a whole town. What it was missing was a long road to follow, a clear answer to "what am I working toward?" This update adds that road, plus a new kind of mine and a better way to meet the minigames.

The Grand Journey

From Stage 2 there's a JOURNEY button on the map. It opens a painted Roman Road with 20 stops, and each stop is a task: mine a number of gems, beat bosses, play trail minigames, open packs, win in Brawl, upgrade your town and so on.

I made one small choice that I really like. Finishing a task doesn't pay you straight away. It earns the stop, and then Tronchetto walks down the road to it, and the reward comes when he arrives. It's a tiny moment, but it makes progress feel like a trip instead of a checklist.

The big rewards sit at the milestones: 500 gold at stop 5, 5 Lucky Spins at stop 10 and a Rare+ pack at stop 15. Stop 20 gives the Journey Pack, which holds La Grande Combination, a Legendary character you can only get here. He has his own Brawl kit too.

I'll be honest: the second half of the road is hard on purpose. Stops 12 to 20 ask for things like reaching Stage 30, owning 50 characters, winning 75 Brawl battles and collecting 10,000 gems, and the final stop asks for Stage 50. The Journey is meant to sit in the background for weeks, so there's always a big goal ahead, even after you've beaten the bosses in front of you.

Cave-In: the endless wall

The Gem Mine is the heart of the game, and I wanted a version of it with no finish line. Cave-In is a new trail stop that shows up from Stage 4. It's one long wall of gem bricks, nine bricks wide, built from the mine's wall shapes stacked end to end. The wall creeps down a row every few seconds and speeds up as you go deeper. Your run ends when a brick reaches the red line or when you run out of pickaxes.

The game remembers your best depth, and there are bonuses at 50, 100 and 200 rows. While I was at it, I added five new wall shapes to the normal mine as well: Cross, Crown, Arrow, Smiley and Mushroom.

Minigames that arrive over time

The trails have a lot of minigames, and a new player used to meet all of them at once. Now your trails start with six: Forge Strike, Gem Match, Cannoli Cannon, Gondola Dash, Crew Fishing and Treasure Dig. Then one more joins every two stages, from Gem Polish at Stage 3 to Clock Rhythm at Stage 17, and each one arrives with a small "new minigame" banner. That way every few bosses there's something fresh to try, and you get time to learn each one.

Fewer taps

A few changes are just about respect for your time:

  • FILL & FORGE in the Workshop fills every empty socket with your best gems and forges, in one tap.
  • A stop's banner shows in full the first time, then much more briefly after that. Walks between stops are quicker too.
  • Small news, like a new minigame, now shows as a banner on the map instead of a card you have to close.
  • The strip under the map shows your single next goal, and tapping it takes you there.

I also changed the daily gifts: Day 2 is now a Rare+ pack and Day 7 an Epic+ pack, so coming back the next day is worth it.

What's next

I'm keeping an eye on how long the later Journey stops take real players. If something feels too slow or too grindy, tell me through the contact page.
