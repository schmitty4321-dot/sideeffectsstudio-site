Hatch & Grow: faster coins and a roomier board

November 1, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

On October 31 and November 1 I made two updates to Hatch & Grow. The first changed the board itself. The second made coins come in faster and smoothed out the first few minutes for new players. Here's what changed and why.

A board that's always 20 squares

The board used to start small, with locked squares around the edge that you bought one at a time. In practice that meant the early game was cramped, and the locked squares took up space on screen without doing anything. So I removed them. The board is now always 20 squares: 4 across and 5 down on an upright phone, 5 across and 4 down on a sideways screen. With fewer squares to fit, every square and every character got bigger.

Rubble is now capped at 4 pieces per zone. Later zones used to fill a big part of the board with rocks, coals and ice blocks before you'd hatched a single egg. Four is enough to give you something to tap without blocking your merges.

If you already had a save, the game moves your characters over for you. A character on one of the old squares joins a matching stack if there's room, takes a free square if there isn't, and is sold for coins only if there's no room left at all.

Legends get portraits

Legends are the reason you play, so they should look like it. On the board a Legend now shows as a head-and-shoulders portrait on a gold square, so you can spot them at a glance. The full-body art is still used everywhere else, like the collection strip at the top and the growth guide.

Faster coins

Coins felt too slow. The middle of each zone dragged: the coins came in, but eggs ate them faster than the board could refill them. I made three changes:

  • An egg now costs at most about 15 seconds of your income (it was 25), and an egg of the zone's second character at most about 18 seconds (it was 30). Prices still rise as you buy, but they can't run away from you.
  • An order's reward now keeps up with your income while it waits. Before, an order that sat for a while could end up paying less than the eggs you'd need to fill it.
  • Once the second character is unlocked, more orders ask for it. It earns ten times as much as the first character, so those orders pay more and push you toward the zone's goal.

A friendlier first order, and smaller fixes

The first order you see in the tutorial now asks for a character that's already on your board, so you can drag it over and get paid straight away. Before, it could ask for something you hadn't hatched yet, which was a confusing first lesson.

Babies are now drawn smaller than the older stages. It's a small thing, but it makes growing up feel like growing up, and it's easier to see which squares hold your bigger characters.

I also fixed a few things that got in the way:

  • The Egg Rush label no longer gets covered up.
  • Small phones held sideways get a 5-column board that fits the screen.
  • The Trips and Orders windows no longer pop up over another window you have open.
  • The sounds and the little animations got a polish, and the game uses calmer motion if your device is set to reduce motion.

What's next

I'm watching how long each zone takes now that coins come in faster, and I'll keep tuning prices and order rewards if a zone still drags or flies by too quickly. If something feels off to you, I'd love to hear about it through the contact page.
