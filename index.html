Pocket Exfil

Play free now

Drop in. Grab the loot. Get out alive.

Town Wars

Samurai Runner

Early test: play free

A lone ronin, an ink-painted road, and everything on it wants him dead.

  • Steer with one thumb. Your blades fire on their own; dodge the red arrows, bones and charging riders.
  • Hold a second finger to draw your sword. Time slows. Let go on the white flash for a perfect cut that splits arrows and bamboo.
  • Level up mid-fight with bows, shuriken, talismans and a chain-sickle, then beat the boss at the end of every road.
  • Grow your village between fights: weapon trees at the forge, training at the dojo, and spirit companions at the shrine.

DigDeeper

Early test: play free

Grab a shovel. Dig down. There's no bottom.

  • Dig through eight layers of rock, from soft topsoil to crystal caverns and magma.
  • Hold toward a rock to mine it. 15 minerals from coal to diamonds, and rare glittering veins that get richer the deeper you go.
  • Dig left for metals, right for gems. The mine is 100 columns wide with your camp in the middle.
  • Haul it home and sell it, smelt bars, and upgrade your picks, pack, lamp and lift.
  • Rope up and light the way. Shoot a grapple rope to climb back out, and hang lanterns that light your tunnels for good.

Coin Cavern

Early test: play free

Ten rooms piled ten feet deep in coins. Each client lost one thing somewhere in the pile.

  • Dig with a tap. Scoop coins into your bin, from bare hands to a mug, a bucket and a shovel.
  • Feed the machine. Every coin rides an overbuilt sorting machine (a sewing-room sorter, a spoon cascade, then the big vault machine with brushes, a weigh scale and plinko) and sells when it lands.
  • No x-ray, no detectors. Nobody knows where it is. Look-alikes get stamped FAKE!, and odd treasures go in your photo book.
  • Build it up with a pulley, a conveyor, a coin wrapper, an apprentice digger and a bank.

Hoarder House

Early test: play free

One very full house. Clear it, pack it, clean it, one room at a time.

  • Dig into the pile. Drag each thing off the heap and drop it on the furniture where it belongs: green means its best spot.
  • Pack every drawer like a puzzle. Items have real shapes; turn them and fit them together until a drawer locks with a cha-ching.
  • Scrub it clean with a mop, sponge, spray and duster, then see the before and after.
  • Find the family heirloom hidden at the bottom of every room, and get a grade from A+ to F.
  • The kitchen and the pantry are open now. No time limit, no ads, no real money.

HEXFIL

Early test: play free

Sneak in. Loot. Get out before your deck runs out.

  • Your deck is the clock. Every move, search and shot comes from your cards. When the deck is empty you start bleeding out.
  • Sneak past guards on a hex map of an enemy camp: stay out of their sight cones, pick locks, hack terminals and raid a three-floor HQ.
  • Card fights where you always see the enemy's next move and every card left in its deck.
  • Four bosses and a rival crew raiding the same camp for the same loot.
  • Build your operator at home base: gear, insurance, stats, a skill tree, card upgrades and daily jobs from the War Room. No ads, nothing to buy.
