How to play

Drive your tractor over a field to plow it, then plant it, fertilize it if you like, and come back to harvest when the crop is ripe. Haul each load to the silo, then sell it at the market or use it to fill deals. Spend what you earn on more land, animals, machines and buildings in the city, or hire a farmhand to help.

Tips

  • Market prices move with the seasons and the news. A big sale pushes the price down, so sell in smaller lots when you can.
  • Your farm starts with a free Mill in the city. Making flour can earn more than selling the wheat.
  • Storms get a warning the day before, so bring in ripe crops first.

Play it now

Pocket Acres runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download, and your progress saves automatically on your device.
