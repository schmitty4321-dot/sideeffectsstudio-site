Pet Helpers, tapping tools and a faster start

October 24, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

Tap Tap Sahur is a game about one thing above all: tapping Tronchetto. So for this update I spent most of my time on the tap screen itself. Tronchetto now has tools to swing, a pet to keep him company, and a new drum on the reels. I also cut the wait before your first tap, because a game about tapping shouldn't make you wait to tap.

Six tapping tools

Tronchetto used to have one bat, forever. Now there's a Customize shop (open it from the Wardrobe in the Packs shop) with six tools that you keep for good once you buy them. Each one changes how tapping feels, not just how it looks:

  • Wooden Bat: the free one you start with.
  • Iron Mallet (10,000 credits): 20% more energy per tap.
  • Espresso Hammer (20,000): your frenzy bar fills 25% faster.
  • Gem Pickaxe (40,000): 40% more energy from a charged swing.
  • Golden Drumstick (90,000): every tenth tap gives triple energy.
  • Moonforged Bat (200,000): 35% more tap energy and 20% more frenzy. You can only buy it after you've travelled to your second town.

I wanted tools to be a choice, not a ladder. Fast tappers will love the Espresso Hammer, and people who like to hold for a big charged swing will want the Pickaxe. One thing to know: tools work while Tronchetto wears his classic look.

Pet Helpers

The Hatchery already gave you pets with small bonuses, but they lived on a menu. Now you can pick one pet as your active helper, and it comes out to play. It paces and hops around Tronchetto's feet and darts out to grab the pizza slices, coffee cherries and bubbles that land nearby. Every pet has its own way of catching: the frog uses its tongue, the kitten pounces, and the owl swoops.

I made a few rules so the pet helps without taking over. It never taps Tronchetto for you, so tapping is still your job. Your own grab always comes first. Golden visitors are yours too, except for the little golden dragon, Draghetto, once it reaches level 3. And the pet slowly adds energy to your roll meter while you're on the tap screen: more for rarer pets, and a bit more with each pet level.

Bonus Roll Drums

There's a new symbol on the reels. Land three, four or five Bonus Roll Drums and you bank 2, 4 or 6 extra rolls. They're saved at the Roll Power and multiplier you won them at, so a lucky hit on a big roll stays big.

A quicker, calmer start

Testing the first session of play showed me two problems. The opening was too slow, and then too much happened at once.

So the TAP TO PLAY button is gone. The splash screen leaves by itself as soon as the game is ready, and your first tap on Tronchetto also turns the sound on. The free starter crate now opens after your first roll instead of before you can play. On phones, you can now tap Tronchetto a lot sooner after the page opens.

I also spread out the new features. Before, eight features opened in the first four minutes and then nothing happened for a while. Now something new opens every few minutes: Patapim's Garden after 65 rolls, the Hatchery after 100, and so on. And Tronchetto doesn't get tired at all during your first 15 minutes of play, so a new player never hits a wall right when the game is getting fun.

Smaller fixes went in too. The game copes better with wide screens, small text got bigger on phones, and auto-spin now waits politely while a pack is open.

What's next

I keep tuning the pace so the full collection takes about a month. If you have ideas, or you find a bug, my contact details are on the contact page. Happy tapping.
