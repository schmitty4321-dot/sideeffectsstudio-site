Town Wars: your real town, now with battles and daily quests

November 7, 2026 · by Dr.Dev

Town Wars went up as an early test on November 4. The idea is simple: pick your real town, and it becomes a cartoon kingdom inside its real border, with your castle on the town hall. That first version was a quiet solo kingdom: claim spots, tap to collect, build up. In the week since, it has grown a lot. Here's what changed and why.

Every spot has a gang

Just claiming a spot felt flat, so now every unclaimed spot is held by a gang. Geese guard the fields, raccoons the woods, a Duck Mafia the water, seagulls the shops and a Pigeon Council the landmarks. There are 30 gangs now, each with a boss, and everyone playing the same town meets the same gang on the same spot. You send your army on an expedition, and the spot card tells you plainly whether you'd win.

At first battles resolved on their own. Soon after launch I changed that so you can watch and fight: tap Rally on the beat, fire each troop's special move once (my favorite is the Catapult Grannies' Casserole Barrage), or retreat to save your troops. You can still auto-resolve, and easy fights can resolve by themselves if you like.

Building on real places

On November 6 I moved most buildings out of the castle and onto the map. Only six stay inside the walls. Everything else is built where it belongs: a farm on a field, a lumber mill in the woods, a Food Depot on a supermarket, a Post Office on the post office. Libraries, diners, sports grounds and theatres unlock new fighters, like Llama Lancers and Lunch Lady Archers. I keep schools, homes, playgrounds, medical sites and places of worship off the map entirely.

The same day, nearby towns started showing up as colored neighbor kingdoms. For now you can only look at them. Raids between towns are the big feature still to come.

Daily quests and a kind streak

On November 7 I added three daily quests, a chest for finishing all three, a weekly board and a play streak. Everyone in the same town gets the same quests that day. I made the streak forgiving on purpose: one missed day a week is covered, and missing more halves the streak instead of wiping it. When I tested the quests I found many rewards went unclaimed because stores were full, so now anything that doesn't fit turns into gold.

Slower, fairer pacing

Testing showed the kingdom grew too fast while you were away. So time away now pays half, and your buildings fill up instead, waiting for you to tap them. Bigger castle upgrades now take real time: two hours for Castle 3, three days for Castle 4 and twelve for Castle 5. A test in a small farm town also exposed a food trap: a new army could eat all the food the castle made, so the first expedition never left. The spot card now offers to buy the missing food with gold.

Location, plainly

You can pick your town by typing its name, with no location at all. "Find my town" sends a point rounded to about a kilometer to OpenStreetMap's town lookup, once, and doesn't save it. Walking to real places is an optional switch, off unless you turn it on, and your precise location stays on your phone. The guide's parent section has the details.

What's next

This is still a test build. Raids between towns, real defenses for your Walls and better ways to find your neighbors are the next big pieces, and the numbers will keep changing as I learn how people play. If you try it in your town, tell me what your gangs were.
